# Executive Summary: 6G Network Performance Impact on Smart Manufacturing

## Project Overview
This executive summary provides government and industrial stakeholders with strategic insights into the impact of 6G ultra-low latency communication networks on smart factory efficiency, operational speed, and manufacturing defect rates.

## Key Findings
1. **Latency Thresholds**: Operational efficiency remains stable when 6G slicing maintains network latency below 15 ms. Spikes beyond 35 ms cause automated delay loops.
2. **Packet Loss & Reliability**: Packet loss exceeding 2.0% triggers operational error spikes in critical manufacturing cycles.
3. **Strategic Network Slicing**: Slicing allocation should prioritize high-load active machinery over steady-state background processes.

## Policy Recommendations for Stakeholders
- Mandate ultra-reliable low-latency communication (URLLC) standards for high-precision manufacturing zones.
- Support industrial infrastructure upgrades toward dedicated private 6G network slicing.
